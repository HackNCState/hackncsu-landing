Why Join Us?

We're looking for people who genuinely want to build something worthwhile for the campus. You'll take on actual responsibility, work with students outside your major, and learn by doing. We know you're a student first, so our workflow is flexible. In return, we just ask for consistent effort and communication. If you put in the work, you'll walk away with real experience for your resume, a great team, and yes, a free hoodie.
